PromptShield

I attack chatbots so you don’t get attacked.

An automated red-team for chatbot system prompts: 25 attacks across instruction override, prompt extraction, role-play jailbreaks, obfuscation and indirect injection, graded A to F. Leaks are proven objectively with proof tokens, hidden canaries and seven-word overlap checks — an LLM judge is only the fallback. Then it rewrites the defence, shows the diff, and re-runs everything.
